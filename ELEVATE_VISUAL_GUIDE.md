## Usage Examples

### Reading a Profile

**"CONSCIOUS_SUBSTRATE (CARETAKER-ENGINEER)"**

Translation:
- **CONSCIOUS_SUBSTRATE** = Level 4 (self-aware, spontaneous)
- **CARETAKER-ENGINEER** = Primary drifts (nurturing + building)
- **Hyphen** = Multiple strong drifts (not pure specialist)

### Understanding Drift Patterns

**Single Drift:** "AUTONOMOUS (SCIENTIST)"
- Clear specialization
- 0.7+ drift in one dimension
- Other dimensions 0.3 or below

**Multi Drift:** "CONSCIOUS (CARETAKER-ENGINEER-COGNITION)"
- Balanced development
- 0.4-0.6 in multiple dimensions
- No single dominant trait

**Neutral:** "CONTEXTUAL (Neutral)"
- No significant drift
- All dimensions below 0.3
- Pure general intelligence
